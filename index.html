FAQ

Clear answers before you upgrade.

Is my browsing data uploaded?

Tracking stays local by default. Optional sync (Pro) stores settings and daily aggregates after you sign in — not page content or passwords.

Why does checkout show a higher total than $4.99 / $39 / $79?

Base prices stay $4.99 / $39 / $79. Checkout may add applicable tax (18% GST) so the amount due is shown before you pay. INR plans use USD × INR_USD_RATE (default 95.12) rounded to whole rupees — currently ₹475 / ₹3,710 / ₹7,514, tax included.

Which payment methods are supported?

PayPal and Razorpay (cards / UPI). Card details stay with the payment provider — we never store them.

What happens when my plan ends?

Monthly and yearly Pro end on the expiry date. Lifetime does not expire. After expiry you return to Free limits.

Do I need a license key?

No. After payment, open the extension with the same billing email — Pro unlocks automatically.
